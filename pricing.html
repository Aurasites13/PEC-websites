Cancellation & Reschedulingキャンセル・日程変更

  • Up to 24 hours before a session or meeting: cancel or reschedule freely, with no session consumed.授業・面談開始24時間前まで：キャンセル・日程変更可、回数消化なし
  • Within 24 hours, or a no-show: one session is consumed regardless of reason.24時間以内または無断欠席：理由を問わず1回分消化
  • No monthly limit on changes made 24+ hours in advance.24時間前までの変更回数に月間上限なし
  • Changes or cancellations do not extend the validity period.変更・キャンセルによる有効期限延長なし

Validity有効期限

  • Teaching (6 or 12 sessions): valid for 6 months from purchase, as a general rule.Teaching 6回／12回：購入日から原則6か月有効
  • International Education Advisory: valid for 6 months, as a general rule.International Education Advisory：原則6か月有効
  • Swiss Education Advisory: valid for 6 months, as a general rule.Swiss Education Advisory：原則6か月有効

  • Swiss Post-Enrollment Concierge: 12-month annual contract.Swiss Post-Enrollment Concierge：12か月年間契約

Cancellation & Refunds途中解約・返金について

  • Once a contract is formed and service has begun, PEC service fees are, as a general rule, non-refundable for customer-initiated mid-term cancellation, withdrawal, change of school, leave of absence, return home, or similar circumstances.契約成立・サービス開始後、顧客都合による途中解約・退会・退学・転校・休学・帰国等について、PECサービス料金は原則返金なし
  • Refunds of school tuition, dormitory fees, deposits, etc. follow each institution's own policies; PEC does not guarantee refunds of school-related costs.学校の授業料・寮費・Deposit等の返金は各教育機関の規定に従い、PECは学校費用の返金を保証しない
  • If PEC is unable to continue providing service for reasons on its side, the treatment of the unprovided portion follows the Terms of Service and the contract.PEC側の事情でサービス提供が継続できない場合の未提供部分の取扱いは、利用規約・契約書に従う
